# Walker identity study

Created 27 October 2026 with the built-in image-generation tool. Concept only;
not a sprite atlas, rig, approved final design or packaged runtime asset.

`identity-v01.png` uses the existing sentry only as a material reference and the
Breach floor as a world/perspective reference. Its own long-legged silhouette,
ram chassis and separate weapons follow the creator's new mechanical direction.
Exploded views illustrate intent; their proportions/part boundaries still need
production validation. See docs/MECHANICAL_BOSS_WORKSHOP.md for the staged plan.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: first identity concept proof for ONE modular walking mechanical boss in Regun, a landscape top-down shooter. This is a design proposal, not finished animation frames.
Input reference 1 (small sentry): material language only: aged dark olive iron, bronze joints, heavy industrial construction. DO NOT enlarge, trace, or duplicate its chassis or short squat legs.
Input reference 2 (actual arena floor): match this world's gritty detailed pixel-art surface, light, perspective and restrained palette.
Create one highly legible landscape concept board of a DISTINCT robot with FOUR substantially LONGER articulated hydraulic legs and a compact suspended armored chassis. The walking weapons platform should have generous open space between the body and the feet, long separated upper/lower links, exposed chrome pistons, obvious large knee and hip bearings, broad gripping feet. Its own identity: a forward wedge-like breaching nose/melee ram, an offset independently swiveling rotary gun on one shoulder and a heavy cannon on the other, a compact dorsal missile rack, rear/downward rocket thrusters that could propel a braced charge. Small red targeting optics, muted ochre industrial markings; restrained functional emissions only. Readable bold silhouette first, believable hard-surface detail second. Each assembly visibly attaches at a real joint.
Composition: left two thirds show ONE large fully assembled robot at the actual game's elevated 3/4 top-down view, grounded on a patch of reference arena stone, lit compatibly. Right third shows THREE clean isolated mechanical studies of THIS SAME robot: (1) one long leg showing hip/thigh/knee/shin/foot and piston joints separated slightly along their real assembly, (2) the chassis with gun modules lifted off their pivot sockets, (3) side silhouette comparison standing high versus crouched low to brace for rocket charge. Keep consistent design across all studies. Use unadorned charcoal background around studies. Only small concise captions: "WALKER / 01", "LEG ASSEMBLY", "WEAPON MOUNTS", "BRACE".
Medium: crisp detailed game pixel-art / textured sprite concept, deliberately resolved edges and material shading, not blurry brushwork, not vector primitives or clean gradient shapes, not glossy 3D toy. Strong enough source detail to be shown as a large boss. No generic humanoid mecha face, no organic insect body, no wings, no spider abdomen, no magic circles, no glowing ground telegraphs, no game HUD. Keep every part and leg fully within frame. The main image must immediately read as an original long-legged armored walking siege machine rather than a scaled-up stationary sentry.
