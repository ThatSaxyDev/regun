## Design Context

### Users
Mobile players of Regun, a landscape twin-stick survival shooter for iOS and Android. The player must move, aim, dash, reload, and pick upgrades quickly without controls hiding threats.

### Brand Personality
Dark, tactile, arcade. This direction follows the creator's supplied pixel-art gameplay screenshots and explicit rejection of the unrelated pastel purple/red buttons and oversized A/B controls.

### Aesthetic Direction
Match the existing dark pixel-art arena and shotgun character. Use charcoal/plum metal surfaces, warm brass/ammo accents, sharp stepped corners, and existing PressStart2P lettering. Avoid candy-coloured rounded buttons, large fake controller letters, heavy opaque joystick sprites, glow, and decorative gradients. Preserve shotgun shell timing. The creator now requests a unified mechanical reload presentation instead of the old Lottie (weapon feedback pass, 19 October 2026). The complete-game scope authorizes coherent new sprites and directional animations; retain the character identity and improve readability.

### Design Principles
- Gameplay visibility and thumb reach come first; compact artwork must retain usable hit areas.
- Actions use meaningful dash/reload symbols, not A/B mappings.
- One visual language spans gameplay controls, menu, pause, upgrades and results.
- Use clear pressed, disabled, reloading and active states; do not depend on colour alone.
- Verify actual rendered game and overlay images before reporting the visual work complete.

### Product copy
Keep player-facing text brief and useful. Do not explain engine behavior, clocks, reload preservation, or why combat waits during upgrade selection. Upgrade cards state effects; the player can see the slowed world without a narration line. The creator explicitly rejected that explanatory copy.

### Control layout constraint
Dash and reload belong on the aiming side. Keep the movement thumb on the movement stick during a dash. The creator explicitly rejected moving dash beside movement: adjust reach and spacing without changing this arrangement. Mirror the whole layout for left-handed use.

### Settings controls
Do not ship an unstyled Material switch in the game. Handedness uses a stepped Right / Left selector with a persistent selected marker. Settings sliders use the same charcoal/brass palette and custom pixel grips. Match the finished game UI in pressed, selected, and keyboard-focus states.


### Player and environment replacement direction (19 October 2026)
The creator explicitly rejects the current player sprite and wants it replaced entirely. Do not preserve its body/head/silhouette or recycle its animation frames as the finished replacement. Existing Rook/Scrapper/Warden gameplay roles remain; the visual designs can be fresh. The old map is also rejected; preserve movement readability through authored surface detail, landmarks and shadows instead of retaining its visible grid. See `docs/REGUN_ART_OVERHAUL_PLAN.md`. This supersedes earlier guidance to retain the old character identity.
