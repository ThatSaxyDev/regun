# Fifteen-weapon phone review

21 October 2026. All fifteen primaries are selectable in **Training → Weapon**.
Normal runs retain unlock progression; the ten later additions unlock after
5–30 lifetime settled boss victories. Training never grants those unlocks.
The shipped online lobby still offers its four supported originals. New native
weapons remain gated until authoritative online implementations are negotiated.

Aim Assist works in solo play: **Settings → Aim assist**. It nudges toward a
nearby enemy in the aimed direction, within this weapon's effective range and
clear sight. It does not lock on or shoot through cover. For a direct comparison,
try the same weapon with the setting Off and On. Grenade aiming stays independent.

| Weapon | What to exercise |
|---|---|
| Pump Shotgun | Three pellets, 3.2-second base full reload; existing Lottie. |
| Carbine | Straight rounds with small heading variation; partial/empty reload. |
| Revolver | Heavy precision shots; full reload commitment. |
| Arc Caster | Six charges, two chain jumps; full reload commitment. |
| Burst Rifle | Track during three separate rounds; release cancels unissued rounds. Partial reload 1.4s, empty 2s. |
| Rail Lance | Hold through .45s charge; release early to cancel; line up three bodies. |
| Rivet Driver | Plant rivets, reload to trigger the short fuse, or let the 3s fuse expire. Payloads remain where a dead host fell. |
| Cinder Hose | Sweep the moving flame stream and watch its tail travel/fade after release. Fuel, sustained sound and one-second innate burn stay bounded. |
| Reaper Disc | Aim the outbound pass, then move to set the fixed return point. Each body may be hit once per leg. |
| Scrap Mortar | Hold/drag/release: aim-stick strength chooses landing distance; check cover, delayed landing and blast. |
| Belt-fed Repeater | Listen during .4s spin-up; 70 rounds at faster .085s cadence; releasing unwinds. Test long firing and the heavy box reload. |
| Frost Shotgun | Three simultaneous pellets. Ordinary mobile enemies freeze .6s, then recover .2s and resist refresh for 1.2s after thaw. Bosses/planted roles resist freeze. |
| Prism Cutter | First-body beam interception; HEAT rises, cools after .4s idle, and full heat starts a vent. Reload manually to vent. |
| Spore Lobber | Hold/drag/release chooses a near/far landing. A capsule ruptures into a 2.5s grounded patch. Same-owner patches apply the strongest tick, not stacked ticks. |
| Longwatch Sniper | Hold to aim, release to fire. Five rounds, 12 damage and innate piercing; weak bodies should fall in one shot. |

For each new weapon, try partial/empty reload, held aim during reload, pause and
resume, restart, background/foreground and Continue. Extended Tube still gives
one pickup refill. Check the left-handed layout and Reduced Motion too. Unsupported
projectile upgrades are omitted for beam, cone, mortar, spore modes;
shared damage, utility and appropriate reload/capacity upgrades remain available.

Physical review remains necessary for sprite/hand alignment, cue timing, speaker
sound, warning readability, comfort, balance and sustained performance. Automated
combat results and installation do not establish those approvals.

## Universal tactical reload and sniper fatigue — 22 October 2026

See [RELOAD_AND_SNIPER_FATIGUE.md](RELOAD_AND_SNIPER_FATIGUE.md). Every current
weapon now has a70%-duration ammo-remaining manual reload without its final closing
action/audio; empty reloads retain full timing. Sniper sight and actual shot share
subtle.18→.85° fatigue over six seconds, with active rest recovery. The separate
[SIX_WEAPONS_AND_WEIGHT_PLAN.md](SIX_WEAPONS_AND_WEIGHT_PLAN.md) proposes six stronger
weapons and0–7% walking penalties. Those six and weapon weight are not implemented.
